New to Thai?

Complete beginner? Start here.

Glyphraid starts from the very first letter. All 44 consonants and 34 vowels and tones, each with clear audio.

Then 1008 cards of real words across 33 themes: greetings, numbers, food, getting around, shopping, health, work and more. Works offline. Nothing to set up, no decks to build.

Mastery

It tracks how well you actually know each word.

Every card climbs five levels, based on your correct answers and the days between them.

Three clean sessions in a row make a card Familiar. Get it right again a day later, it is Mastered. Three days after that, Proficient. And losing a run never touches this: mastery does not go backwards.

Question types

It asks harder as you get better.

Three ways a card can be asked:

Read

See the Thai, answer the meaning.

Listening

Hear the word, answer the meaning.

Reverse

See the meaning, type it in Thai.

The training wheels come off on their own. A new card gives you four choices to pick from. Once that card reaches Familiar, the choices disappear and you type the answer yourself.
