How it plays

The words you know are your attack.

Every monster on screen is holding a Thai card. Answer it and you strike. Miss it and it hits you back.

No tapping through. There is no way to deal damage except actually knowing the word.
Runs, relics and dungeons are all in there for one reason: to make daily review something you want to come back to.
Lose a run, keep everything you learned. Your card mastery never goes backwards.

The whole writing system, then the words you'll actually use.

Why it sticks

Spaced repetition, without building your own decks.

Every card you meet goes into your library and climbs five levels of mastery. A card only moves up when you get it right again on a later day.

You meet each word again just as you are about to forget it. That is what moves it into long term memory instead of letting it fall out overnight.
